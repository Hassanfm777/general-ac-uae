Three ways to install

Capacities from 18,000 to 60,000 Btu/h.

Wall mounted

18,000 to 36,000 Btu/h. Long-reach airflow of up to 25 m on the ASGA series, with a fast-cooling inverter option.

Duct

18,000 to 60,000 Btu/h. Concealed in the ceiling for large homes and multi-outlet spaces, with adjustable static pressure up to 150 Pa on selected models.

Cassette

18,000 to 54,000 Btu/h. Circular airflow that reaches every corner of shops and offices.
